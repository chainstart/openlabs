# OpenLabs biology and bioinformatics papers

Use the existing paper registry, claim–evidence map, manuscript, reviews and release state.
Read `profiles.yaml`, `../overlays/quality-gate.md`, registry settings and the paper record.
Read [paper-identifiers.md](../references/paper-identifiers.md) for manuscript/export identifiers.
Load `../vendor/scientific-writing/SKILL.md`; load statistical-analysis only when statistical
design or calculations are in scope, and peer-review only in the assigned reviewer role.
Use at most the three components declared in the profile.

1. Recover the biological/computational question, precise principal finding, sampling unit,
   paired comparisons, strongest prior results and the claim–evidence relationships before editing.
   Apply the shared readability overlay. Distinguish computational consistency, biological truth,
   analytical validity, empirical generalization and clinical usefulness.
2. Inspect real sample identity and provenance, selection timing, public-data access conditions,
   ethics, licenses, dependencies, coordinate frames and data exclusions. Synthetic reads and
   repeated conditions are not independent biological samples. Do not fabricate human checks,
   historical model identifiers, consent, ethics approval or final author attestations.
3. Verify outcome denominators, uncertainty appropriate to the actual unit, dependencies,
   post-hoc choice/search, held-out validation and matched baselines. Negative accuracy results
   and residual discrepancies belong in the manuscript and review packet. Limit conclusions
   when evidence concerns one subject, regional pools or an artificial error distribution.
4. Use code-first support packaging and the configured Zenodo lifecycle. Cite the current
   Version DOI, retain necessary third-party licenses and accession/version instructions,
   and distinguish checksum/summary replay from independent raw-data reanalysis.
5. Consolidate truthful AI purposes and actual recorded models in one final declaration;
   keep runtime provenance private. Registered author roles, funding and conflicts are
   paper-specific. Run declaration, style, support, input-hygiene and real PDF-build checks.
6. Verify the target's fixed 2025 CAS major-category classification, fee-free route, format,
   current scope, two recent adjacent articles and submission history. A user-approved
   paper/target-specific tier override never lowers the scientific review gate.
7. Run the deterministic review router. A full review uses one fresh independent Codex
   process with `biology`, `openlabs.paper-writing.biology-leading-journals.v1`, and separate
   `leading_life_sciences_journals` / generic `cas_zone_1_journal` judgments. Do not borrow
   conference, mathematics, materials, physics or finance rubrics. Hide all earlier judgments.
8. Resolve every necessary scientific and substantial writing repair within the existing
   round budget. Run the independent target-specific editorial screen on the frozen manuscript.
   Only a validated `writing_release.status: ready` permits support release and immutable
   management-site handoff. Neither AI review nor website registration is journal submission.
