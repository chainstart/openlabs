# OpenLabs biology and bioinformatics paper

Follow the shared workflow in `../openlabs-paper-writing/SKILL.md`
(`workflows/paper/skills/openlabs-paper-writing/SKILL.md` in the code repository). Identifiers
and public file names: [paper-identifiers.md](../references/paper-identifiers.md). Venue,
review and author policy: `registry/settings.yaml`. This file adds only what differs for biology and bioinformatics.

## Biology specifics

- Components: `scientific-writing`; `statistical-analysis` when design or calculations are in
  scope; `peer-review` only as an assigned reviewer.
- Distinguish computational consistency, biological truth, analytical validity, empirical
  generalization and clinical usefulness; claim only the level the evidence reaches.
- Identify the real sampling unit. Synthetic reads and repeated conditions are not independent
  biological samples; limit conclusions drawn from one subject, regional pools or an artificial
  error distribution.
- Check sample provenance, public-data access conditions, ethics, licences, coordinate frames
  and exclusions. Never fabricate consent, ethics approval or human checks.
- Report denominators, uncertainty at the actual unit, held-out validation and matched
  baselines; residual discrepancies stay in the paper.
