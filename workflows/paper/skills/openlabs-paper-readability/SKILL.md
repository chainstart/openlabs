# Writing a paper people read

A reader from the target venue should be able to retell the question, comparison, answer and meaning after one reading.

## The first reading

Use the target journal's article type and official class for the submission proof. Use its abstract fields and citation rules. In the story card name the decision, closest existing answer, decisive comparison and boundary. Point each claim to its figure-plan panel or table row.

- Title the measured object and judgment. Scope the verb to the population and design.
- Build the abstract from shared context to gap, comparison, finding and wider meaning. Give the denominator, unit and baseline for the number that changes the answer.
- Funnel the introduction from an observable consequence through the closest prior answer to the comparison that distinguishes explanations.
- Lead each Results section or paragraph with its finding. Name the panel, estimate, comparator and uncertainty. Open each caption with a title sentence, then decode panels in order, marks, independent unit, group sizes and interval or error definition.
- In Discussion explain whose judgment changes, the closest competing explanation and the limitation that would change the decision.
- In Methods trace the sampling unit through exclusions, preprocessing, estimator and validation to the decisive panel. Give versioned data and code access, including any restrictions. Cite supplementary figures or sections at the claims they support.
- Ask what a reader would misunderstand if each qualifier disappeared. Translate internal criteria into observable scientific implications. Read the compiled proof at print size for broken equations, references and figure labels.

For calibration, consult the [two annotated research articles](references/article-readings.md).

## Revision

Change the main text where a comparison, magnitude or condition changes the conclusion.
Put secondary scientific analyses in the accompanying supplement, replay logs and hashes
in the reproducibility archive, and editorial correspondence in the response. Aim for zero
net growth by cutting a detour when adding a necessary explanation. Preserve negative
results and registered endpoints. Narrative diagnostics and the author’s reader-check are
optional. The independent delivery reader reconstructs question, comparison, answer and
meaning from the opening, figures and conclusion.
