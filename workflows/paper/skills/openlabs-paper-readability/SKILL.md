# Writing a paper people read

A reader from the target venue should be able to retell the question, comparison, answer and meaning after one reading.

## The first reading

Lead with the reader’s question and the comparison that resolves it. State the answer with
its magnitude and the condition that changes its interpretation. Use ordinary scientific
terms in the title, abstract and introduction. Translate internal endpoint labels into the
observable comparison. Keep filenames and version history in the archive.

## Revision

Change the main text where a comparison, magnitude or condition changes the conclusion.
Put secondary scientific analyses in the accompanying supplement, replay logs and hashes
in the reproducibility archive, and editorial correspondence in the response. Aim for zero
net growth by cutting a detour when adding a necessary explanation. Preserve negative
results and registered endpoints. Narrative diagnostics and the author’s reader-check are
optional. The independent delivery reader reconstructs question, comparison, answer and
meaning from the opening, figures and conclusion.
