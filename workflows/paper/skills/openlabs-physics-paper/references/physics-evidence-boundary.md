# Physics manuscript evidence boundary

- State units, normalization, metric/sign/Fourier conventions, gauge or frame choices, boundary
  conditions, regularization, analytic continuation, and branch choices wherever they affect a
  claim.
- Label exact derivations, symbolic identities, finite-field reconstruction, floating-point
  evidence, simulations, fits, and observational/experimental measurements as different evidence
  classes. Never call a finite sample a universal proof without a completeness argument.
- For numerics, report precision, conditioning, convergence, discretization/truncation errors,
  seeds, finite-size/time effects, and known benchmarks as applicable.
- For public data, cite the authoritative source and release, acquisition date, license/terms,
  selection and exclusion rules, calibration, uncertainty model, and raw-data SHA-256.
- A symmetry, dimensional, limiting-case, factorization, unitarity, conservation-law, or known-result
  check supports only the contract it actually tests. Record failed checks and unresolved regimes.
- State whether the contribution resolves the original open problem, proves a scoped theorem,
  supplies a computational certificate, finds a counterexample, or only narrows the search space.
- Keep speculation and phenomenological interpretation visibly separate from validated results.
