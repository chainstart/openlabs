# Quantitative-finance evidence gates

## Data gate

- Source URL/provider, terms, retrieval time, file hash, coverage and version are recorded.
- Every predictor is available before the declared decision time; revisions use the correct vintage.
- Universe membership, delistings, survivorship, corporate actions and missing observations are explicit.
- Train, validation and sealed confirmation periods are chronological and transformation fitting is local.

## Search gate

- The trial ledger contains failed, null and superseded routes, not only the selected result.
- Hypothesis families, parameter ranges, metrics, stopping rules and selection criteria are registered.
- Cross-validation respects temporal dependence, embargo/purge needs and the actual statistical unit.
- Selection-adjusted uncertainty or a justified multiple-testing correction covers the searched family.

## Backtest gate

- Trades occur after information arrival, with a declared lag and market calendar.
- Fees, spread, slippage, funding/borrow, turnover, leverage and capacity match the intended market.
- Gross and net results, exposure, drawdown and benchmark-relative effects are separately reported.
- Execution-sensitive findings receive event-driven or otherwise independent implementation validation.

## Scientific promotion gate

- The closest published methods and strong simple baselines use matched information and budgets.
- The effect survives reasonable periods, regimes, universes and nuisance specifications.
- Mechanism tests discriminate the proposed explanation from plausible alternatives.
- A fresh reproduction rebuilds claim-bearing tables from frozen data, code, environment and seeds.
- The paper claim is no broader than the verified evidence and names unresolved external-validity limits.
