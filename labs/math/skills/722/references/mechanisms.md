# Mechanism selection and reconstruction

These are optional diagnostics, not a fixed proof recipe.

| Obstacle | Representation to consider | Test before further investment |
|---|---|---|
| Sequential precision losses overwhelm the desired bound | Shared structure, simultaneous alignment, entropy or moment accounting | Recompute the entire cost including normalization and sparse exceptional pieces |
| Local compatibility fails to assemble globally | Joint witnesses, incidence structures, coupling or algebraic reconstruction | Small incompatible configurations; identify the unproved compatibility lemma |
| Absolute values destroy signed cancellation | Retain signs through integration; separate structural and centered variables | Repeated labels, conditional dependence, and return edges |
| An invariant forgets needed information | Add a second invariant or change the encoded object | Construct a family with the same invariant but different target behavior |
| Arithmetic height defeats analytic decay | Track divisibility, nonvanishing and denominator cost together | Liouville-type comparators and degenerate parameter choices |
| Universal proof routes stall | Construct an explicit obstruction in a smaller coefficient field or model | Certify every equality and the surviving nonzero witness |

For a mechanism borrowed from another field, separate inspiration from a theorem dependency.
State exactly how the objects, hypotheses and conclusion transfer. Preserve the gain/loss ledger
even for a promising route; no step called “routine” can hide the original theorem-strength gap.

Review a frozen candidate by attempting to break its critical interface and then reconstructing it.
Check all quantifiers, extremizers and degeneracies; what is controlled (mean, law, pointwise or
uniformly); conditioning and independence; preservation of nonzero witnesses under specialization;
and the order of every limit. A repeated self-audit is not an independent reconstruction.

Count source-problem resolutions, precise intermediate theorems, eliminated gaps, reusable
mechanisms, reconstruction survival and total measured usage. Document production and negative
finite searches are not mathematical success metrics.

[public reasoning summaries](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/README.md#reasoning-summaries).
These are abridged traces of selected examples, not a complete or causally validated recipe.
