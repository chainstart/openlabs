# Phase gates

## State transitions

| Current phase | Required output before advancing |
|---|---|
| `target_selection` | Exact source-original statement, separately frozen local target, typed target relation, primary source and published comparator, frozen success and non-success conditions, an explicit false-world control, and for an exact target a hash-bound primary-source selection bundle |
| `obstruction_analysis` | At least one inherited method with exact loss step, lost information, and consequence |
| `representation_search` | Configured minimum of valid representations and mechanisms across distinct families |
| `mechanism_falsification` | Evidenced kill tests, configured kill ratio, and one to three survivors |
| `survivor_deepening` | A proved decisive theorem/lemma with exact scope, closure effect, unconditional inputs, non-cosmetic consequence, evidence, and no unresolved dependency gaps |
| `independent_audit` | Commit the proposed promotion decision, prepare the author-byte review manifest, and obtain blind reconstruction plus hostile statement, dependency, counterexample, novelty, and promotion-decision checks; promotion then requires a fresh reviewer result bound to that manifest and verified against the control-plane archive and database |

The defaults are eight representations across four families, ten mechanisms across four families, an 80% kill ratio among non-survivors, and at most three survivors. Initialization or explicit migration freezes the effective gate policy in `statement_identity`; editing thresholds afterward fails validation. A materially different policy requires a new campaign.

## Fail-closed rules

- Do not advance when a required field is merely promised.
- Do not treat an author re-reading their own proof as independent reconstruction.
- Do not let a reviewer audit mutable or unspecified bytes: prepare the review manifest after the
  decision, and require the reviewer result to bind its exact SHA-256.
- Do not promote finite computation to an all-parameter theorem without a complete reduction.
- Do not replace the public statement with a local bridge after initialization.
- Do not fill a target-selection comparison quota with special cases or altered statements; every
  counted card must be an exact source-original open problem or conjecture with cleared novelty risk.
- Do not let the selected comparison card drift from the selection receipt: its problem, title,
  source statement, frozen statement, relation, source, status, locator, score, and risk verdict
  must match the receipt that initializes the campaign.
- Do not edit a frozen statement or relation in place; the state and initial history bind their
  SHA-256 identities together with the frozen success type.
- Do not type a specialization, strengthening, or partial target as original-problem closure.
- Do not downgrade an exact source target to scoped promotion; exact targets use
  `original_problem_closed`, and non-exact targets use `scoped_theorem_proved`.
- Carry the same type through `mechanisms[*].would_close` and `decisive_lemma.closes`; every proved
  survivor and proved decisive lemma must name the contract's frozen success condition.
- A conditional or refuted decisive lemma, or any unresolved dependency gap, may be preserved and
  frozen but cannot cross into independent audit or promotion.
- Freeze an exhausted representation; do not create cosmetic variants to satisfy a count.
- Preserve negative results and failed mechanisms as evidence.

## Promotion outcomes

Use exactly one frozen success condition:

- `original_problem_closed`
- `scoped_theorem_proved`
- `main_term_improved`
- `main_exponent_improved`
- `global_interface_closed`
- `standalone_decisive_lemma`

`original_problem_closed` is valid only when the frozen target matches the source-original
statement after whitespace normalization. All non-exact targets promote, if audited, as
`scoped_theorem_proved`. Promotion says the package has crossed its research contract. It does not
by itself claim publication, priority, or peer review.

`validate-integrity` replays the complete mathematical phase gate for a campaign whose durable
phase is `promotion`. A terminal phase label and syntactically valid history without the required
proof and independent-audit artifacts cannot authorize promotion.

Every promotion decision declares `resolution_type` as `proof` or `counterexample`, and the fresh
reviewer result must repeat that polarity and the exact success condition. Original-problem
closure additionally needs a current hash-bound recheck that the public source still presents the
statement as open.
